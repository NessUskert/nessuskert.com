My Academics

My academic interests stretch across the humanities, sciences, mathematics, and technology. I enjoy asking difficult questions, connecting ideas across subjects, and using both analytical and creative thinking to understand the world more deeply.

01

Learning is most meaningful when subjects begin to connect.

I am drawn to classes that require interpretation, problem-solving, discussion, research, and experimentation. Whether I am analyzing a piece of literature, studying a historical conflict, solving a mathematical problem, or building a computer science project, I try to understand not only the answer, but also the reasoning behind it.

How I Learn

My academic process

  1. 01

    Ask a precise question

    I begin by identifying what I actually want to understand rather than immediately searching for an answer.

  2. 02

    Gather competing perspectives

    I look for sources, methods, and interpretations that challenge one another instead of relying on a single explanation.

  3. 03

    Test my understanding

    I write, solve, explain, build, or experiment so that I can find the places where my understanding is still incomplete.

  4. 04

    Revise the question

    The best academic work often produces a more thoughtful question rather than a perfectly final answer.
