Personal Pursuits

My Athletics

Athletics has taught me how to remain disciplined, respond to setbacks, and continue improving even when progress feels slow. Through track and field, strength training, and competition, I have learned how physical growth depends on patience, consistency, and confidence.

Improvement happens one practice at a time.

I compete in track and field and train across several different events. Each event requires its own combination of speed, strength, coordination, technique, and mental focus. Training for multiple disciplines has challenged me to become a more adaptable athlete.
