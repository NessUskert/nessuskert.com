The Beauty of a Teardrop

By Ness Uskert • Poetry

They quench the thirsting drought of your mind, Prismatically holding all sight in an oval body, Leaking out your eye like from a cemetery, spirit.

You fear the desire you have for their presence, Scorning God in a faint prayer when your eyes remain arid, asking, “Why won’t they just come?”
